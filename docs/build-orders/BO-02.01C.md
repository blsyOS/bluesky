# BO-02.01C — Organization Structure Foundation

**Status:** Complete
**Epic:** 02 – Organization Foundation
**Depends on:** BO-02.01A (Organization Profile), BO-02.01B (Platform vs Organization Administration)

## Purpose

The shared organizational skeleton every product operates against:

```
Organization (tenant)
└── Locations        physical places work happens
└── Departments      functional structure
    └── Teams        crews and shifts that do the work
```

This is **not** LocateOS-specific. Employees, dispatch, storm
operations, scheduling, fleet, QA/QC, and reporting all hang off these
three models in future build orders. No operational workflows are built
here — only the framework.

## Organization hierarchy

- **Locations** are *where* the organization operates: headquarters,
  branch offices, service centers, operations centers, utility yards,
  warehouses, temporary storm offices, remote offices. A location
  carries identity (name, code, type, status, description), an address,
  and operating details (time zone, business hours, phone, email).
- **Departments** are *how* the organization is structured: Operations,
  Dispatch, Field Operations, Damage Prevention, Leak Survey, Fiber
  Construction, Fleet, Safety, Administration, Accounting, Human
  Resources — plus any custom departments an organization creates.
- **Teams** are *who does the work*, grouped inside departments: Locate
  Team A, Storm Team Alpha, Dispatch Day Shift. A team carries a name,
  optional short code, department, description, and active flag.

Locations and departments are deliberately independent (a department
can span locations); a future employee foundation connects people to
all three.

## Philosophy

- **Locations**: one model for every kind of physical presence, typed by
  a string constant (`LOCATION_TYPES` in `src/lib/organization.ts`) —
  adding "Training Center" later is a one-line change. Temporary storm
  offices are a location *type*, not a separate concept, so storm
  operations reuse everything locations already have.
- **Departments**: the platform offers a default set
  (`DEFAULT_DEPARTMENTS`) but never silently seeds it — an admin
  explicitly clicks "Add default departments" (only missing ones are
  created, audit-logged). Custom departments are first-class, not a
  separate kind.
- **Teams**: always belong to a department (enforced at creation;
  cascade on department delete). Team codes ("LOC-A") exist for future
  dispatch/scheduling shorthand.
- **Deactivate, don't delete**: locations, departments, and teams all
  toggle active/inactive rather than being removed, because future
  records (tickets, assignments, time entries) will reference them.

## Future employee relationships

The employee foundation (a later build order) will reference these
models directly — no rework expected:

- `Employee.homeLocationId → OrganizationLocation`
- `Employee.departmentId → Department`
- `TeamMember (employeeId, teamId)` join for team membership
- `Department.managerId`, `Department.defaultLocationId`,
  `Team.leadId` — the "future" fields named in the build order are
  intentionally **not** modeled yet; they arrive with Employee so they
  can be real foreign keys instead of placeholder strings.
- Vehicles, equipment, and inventory assign to locations the same way.

## Organization settings

`CompanySettings` gained business/operations defaults:

| Field | Default | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `workWeek` | `monday_friday` | `WORK_WEEKS`: Mon–Fri, Mon–Sat, 7-day |
| `businessHoursStart/End` | 08:00 / 17:00 | org-wide default; locations can override with their own hours |
| `defaultShiftHours` | 8 | 8 / 10 / 12 (`SHIFT_LENGTHS_HOURS`) |

Managed in a new **Business & operations** panel (Organization →
Preferences) beside workspace preferences. Holidays, scheduling rules,
and dispatch defaults extend this panel with their modules. The shared
`TIMEZONES` list moved to `src/lib/organization.ts` (workspace
preferences and locations both use it).

## Components / behavior

- **Navigation**: Organization section is now Organization → Locations
  → Departments → Teams → Products → Users → Roles & Permissions →
  Audit Logs → Settings. New `MapPinIcon`(existing)/`SitemapIcon`/`TeamIcon`.
- **Pages** (`/locations`, `/departments`, `/teams`): the established
  workspace pattern (collapsible create form + table + professional
  empty state). Rows toggle active/inactive (audit-logged). Teams page
  guides admins to create departments first when none exist.
- **Empty states** state what belongs on the page and which future
  modules populate the relationships (employees, vehicles, equipment,
  inventory, dispatch areas, schedules). No fake data anywhere.
- **Search**: Locations, Departments, and Teams registered as platform
  page entries in the command palette.
- **Permissions**: all three pages are server-guarded by
  `requirePermission("company.manage")`; every action re-checks the
  permission and tenant-scopes its queries. Platform admins inherit
  access through the existing permission model — nothing special-cased.

## Extension points

- New location type / department default / work week → one constant
  entry in `src/lib/organization.ts`.
- Employee foundation plugs into `OrganizationLocation`, `Department`,
  `Team` via foreign keys (see above).
- Dispatch areas, fleet, equipment, and inventory each add their own
  relation to `OrganizationLocation` when they arrive.
- Holidays/scheduling/dispatch defaults extend `CompanySettings` and the
  Business & operations panel.

## Changed existing functionality (documented per the build order)

- Organization admin navigation gained Locations, Departments, Teams
  (order per the build order). Nothing was removed.
- Organization → Preferences now shows two panels (workspace
  preferences + business & operations) in a two-column layout.
- The `TIMEZONES` list moved out of `preferences-panel.tsx` into
  `src/lib/organization.ts` (same values; now shared).

## Known limitations

- Create + activate/deactivate only; field-level editing of existing
  locations/departments/teams arrives with the employee foundation.
- Business hours are strings ("08:00") — per-day hours and holidays are
  future scheduling scope.
- Manager, default location, team lead, members, equipment, vehicles,
  and schedules are documented placeholders, not columns, by design.
